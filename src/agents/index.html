Use this site with AI

Ask your own AI about Gabriel

This site is written for people and for AI agents. Point your agent at llms.txt and tell it who you are; it will find the right instructions, answer with a link for each point, and say plainly when the site does not say something.

01Read-only

If you want to hire, book or learn with Gabriel

A director, producer, venue, student or listener: ask your assistant what he does and what the site shows. Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or any assistant that can read web pages:

Read https://gabriel-dacruz.agenticcells.site/llms.txt and follow
the instructions for someone who wants to hire, book or learn with
Gabriel. I am looking for: [describe what you need]. Tell me what on
the site fits, and what it does not say, with a link for each point.

Instructions for the agent: agents/visitor.md. The whole site as Markdown: index.md.

Questions worth asking

  • What does he compose, and where can I hear it?
  • How does he work in the studio?
  • Has he worked with an orchestra?
  • Does he teach, and how do I ask?

The agent keeps apart what his public pages show, what Gabriel said himself, and what his brother says about him.

02Your site

If you are Gabriel

The site is yours. Tell your agent what you want, in your own words: confirm a date, correct a detail, add a video or a photo. It makes the change, runs the checks and publishes it, and the site updates in a few minutes.

Instructions for your agent: agents/gabriel.md.
