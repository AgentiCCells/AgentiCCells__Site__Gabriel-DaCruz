# Questions for Gabriel

What Gabriel's own AI should ask him, one topic at a time, to complete his site. Ask for the specific, not the adjective: which year, which room, how many musicians, what was on the stand. Read back three facts at the end and mark anything he is unsure of as TO CONFIRM.

## To confirm what the site already says

1. The Separate Ways video: a direct link, and whether he is happy for the site to embed it.
2. The Spotify album: its title, its year, and a link. Why are the tracks numbered as movements, with Lua as I. again?
3. Each YouTube video (Live and Let Die, the A-Team theme, #MyStargirlScore): who played, where, and when.
4. The band in the Algarve: its name, the years, where it played.
5. The music school: its name, where, when, and what was taught.
6. The Lisbon film music concert: its name, the venue, the dates, the films, how many musicians.
7. The award his Instagram description mentions: what it was, and when.
8. Photos he is happy to publish: the studio, at the synth, the orchestra, a portrait.

## To add explanations like Separate Ways

9. Another video he could explain the same way: the instrument, the chain, the trick.
10. How he arranges a song for orchestra: what he writes first, and what goes wrong in rehearsal.
11. The studio: what the Mac Studio, Logic and Pro Tools each do in his work.
12. What he teaches, and to whom.

## Who asked him

13. For each piece of work above: who asked him to do it? Being asked is the evidence.
