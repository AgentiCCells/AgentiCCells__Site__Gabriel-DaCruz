How it is run

  • Files in git. The site is a public repository; a change pushed to its dev branch is live in a few minutes.
  • Checked before it goes out. Every change passes a validator (no broken links, no hype words), a leak scan for secrets, and a test of every page in a browser at phone and desktop widths.
  • Gabriel approves. Every change about him goes through his brother, and nothing is published as fact until he confirms it. No credit, date or number is invented.
  • Readable by agents. The facts are in index.md, the works in works.json, and llms.txt says how to use them.

What comes next plan

  • A page for every work, starting from the list of every work.
  • Everything he owns online: recordings, sessions, scores and videos, uploaded raw and listed, so nothing is lost with a computer or an account.
  • A short interview a day, by voice memo, turned into a post he confirms, and his story written one conversation at a time.
  • A weekly brief: what was published, what waits for him, what is stuck.
