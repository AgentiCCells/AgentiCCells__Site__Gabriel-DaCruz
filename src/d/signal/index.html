One take. Three cameras. Explained.

Gabriel played the synth part of Journey's Separate Ways (Worlds Apart), the song that opens Frontiers (single, January 1983), in his studio, filmed it from three angles at once, and then explained what the video shows. This design follows the signal, from the keyboard to the screen.2

CH 02 · INST · keyboard to synth

The chain

No cable, no lag: MIDI over Bluetooth Low Energy sends only small note messages, and its specification requires the connection to update every 15 milliseconds or less; headphones lag because they stream buffered audio. In his words, "there's a different protocol if you want to connect MIDI."2

CHECKED He flagged it himself ("They used that Prophet 5, I think. I have to check that out."): the record is credited to a Roland Jupiter-8, and the Pro-800 copies the Prophet-600.2

CH 04 · CAP · capture

Three old iPhones into one iPad

  • Cameras two iPhone 12s, one iPhone 13, second-hand
  • App Final Cut Camera, free
  • Recorder Final Cut Pro for iPad 2.0, released 20 June 2024
  • Angles up to four; the iPad's camera can be one
  • Files full quality on each phone, transferred afterwards
  • Edit Final Cut Pro on the Mac

"When I discovered this, I was blown away." He found it by accident.3
