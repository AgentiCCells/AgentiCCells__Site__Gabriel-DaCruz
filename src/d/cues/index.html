1M1

Separate Ways, explained

Journey's Separate Ways (Worlds Apart) opens the album Frontiers, a single in January 1983. Gabriel played its synth part in his studio, filmed it from three cameras in one take, then explained it, by voice; every claim was checked.2

GABRIEL"I put this video to show how it works."2

At the end he turns the decay down while playing. Each chord goes staccato; the delay repeats it, softer each time. The checking corrected two details he flagged himself: the record is credited to a Roland Jupiter-8; his Pro-800 copies the Prophet-600.2

1M2

Three cameras, no crew

Two iPhone 12s and an iPhone 13, second-hand, "because they were cheaper, and I just needed the camera", connected wirelessly to an iPad running Final Cut Pro. Live Multicam, released by Apple on 20 June 2024: up to four angles, the iPad's camera can be one; live and in sync; full-quality files from each phone; finished in Final Cut Pro on the Mac.3

GABRIEL"When I discovered this, I was blown away."3
